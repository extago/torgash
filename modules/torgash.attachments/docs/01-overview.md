# Documentation

Files beside a product — a specification sheet, a manual, drawings,
certificates — and links to documents on the maker's own site. They stand in
the product page's **Documentation** section, after the characteristics.

## Adding them

Open a product, tab **Documentation**:

- **Upload files** — one or several at once. Which types are taken and how big
  a file may be are the module's settings. The type is read from the file's
  contents, not its name; programs, web pages and SVG are refused whatever the
  list says.
- **Add a link** — a document the maker keeps up to date on their own site.
  It opens there, in a new tab.

Each row has a **title** (in the content language chosen at the top of the
form; empty — the file's own name is shown) and **who sees it**: everyone, or
signed-in customers only. Drag the rows to change their order. Titles, access
and order are saved with the product's Save button; uploading and removing
happen at once.

## On the product page

A PDF or a picture opens in the browser's own viewer; any other file is
downloaded under its own name. A document for signed-in customers is shown to
a guest as well — with a lock and the way in — so they know it is there.

The files are kept on the shop's **private** disk and handed over only through
the shop: a document of a product that is switched off is nobody's. The number
of times each file was opened from the shop is shown beside it in the form.

The section can be renamed, moved or switched off like any other in
**Catalogue → Product tabs**.
