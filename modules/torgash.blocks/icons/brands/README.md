# Brand icons

From [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 13.21.0 (`cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@13.21.0/icons/`),
released under **CC0 1.0** — free to ship with the engine. The brands themselves
remain their owners' trademarks; the icons are drawn single-colour
(`currentColor`) by the "Social networks" and "Payment logos" blocks.

Not in Simple Icons (removed at the owners' request): Mir, SBP, Maestro and
most local banks — a shop adds those as its own picture
(`Name | /storage/…/logo.svg` in the block).

To add one: download `<slug>.svg` from the same version and add the slug to
`Torgash\Blocks\BrandIcons::SOCIAL` or `::PAYMENT`.
