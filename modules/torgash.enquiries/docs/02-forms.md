# Your own forms

A callback, a question about a product and "tell me when it is back" are forms
the shop has from the start. **Forms** (Content → Forms) is for the ones only
your shop needs: a job application, a wholesale request, a survey after a
delivery.

## What a form is made of

- **Name** — the heading over the form and the enquiry's kind in the inbox.
- **Text above the form** — a line or two of what it is for.
- **Fields**, in the order they are asked. *Name*, *Phone number* and *Email
  address* are the person's own details: the enquiry is filed under them, and if
  a form has a phone or an email, at least one of them must be filled in. The
  rest are answers: a line, several lines, a number, a date, a list, one of
  several, several of several, a tick box, photos.
- **Button** and **After sending** — what the button says and the thank-you that
  replaces the form.
- **Consent** — a box that must be ticked, with your words and a link to the
  policy. The words are kept with every enquiry as they read when it was sent.
- **Where the enquiries go** — an address of its own (instead of the shop's
  usual one) and a manager every enquiry from it is given to.

Like every text in the admin, a form is written in the content language chosen
at the top. Switch to another language and type the same labels and answers in
it; a list's answers are matched line by line, so keep their order.

## Putting it on a page

- In a text — a page, a blog article, an HTML block — write the form's code on a
  line of its own: `{form:wholesale-request}`. The form's screen shows it with a
  Copy button.
- In a zone — Blocks → **Enquiry form**, and choose the form in the list.

What it brings lands in **Enquiries** like any callback: a status, a manager,
a reply that reaches the person by email, automations.

A form that has brought enquiries cannot be deleted — switch it off, and it
disappears from every page it was placed on.

## Photos

The *Photos* field takes pictures only, kept privately with the enquiry. How
many is the Enquiries setting "Photos a customer may attach"; how large is the
Media module's. Documents (PDF and the like) are not accepted.
