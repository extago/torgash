# Subcategories

A category's page shows the categories under it above its products. It is
how a visitor narrows a large branch: "Smartphones" → "Apple" → "iPhone 17
Pro". The grid below holds every product of the branch anyway, so hiding
them takes nothing away from the page.

## How they look

Appearance → Themes → Configure → **Category page**:

- **Subcategories above the products**: tiles with a picture, buttons
  (compact, in two rows, the rest behind "Show N more"), or not shown.
- **Tiles laid out**: one row that scrolls (with arrows and a thin
  indicator, as on the home page), two rows that scroll, or a grid.
- **Picture frame**: a circle, a square with rounded corners, or a card as
  wide as the tile.
- **Picture size**, **A photo from its products**, **How many products**.

The preview opens on a category that has subcategories.

## One category differently

Catalog → Categories → the category → **Subcategories**:

- **On this category's page**: as the theme says, tiles, buttons or not
  shown. For example, buttons for "Apple" with its 26 models and tiles
  everywhere else.
- **Shown among its parent's subcategories**: untick it to leave the
  category out of its parent's tiles. The menus and the list beside the
  catalogue still show it.

## The pictures

A tile shows, in this order:

1. the category's **tile picture** (the category's form, "Icon" card);
2. if it has none and **A photo from its products** is on, the photo of the
   most popular product in that category (subcategories included) that
   can be ordered right now and has a photo; when sales change the leader,
   the picture changes with it;
3. the category's own icon picture or its icon from lucide.dev;
4. the first letter of its name.

A picture you upload always wins over a borrowed one.
