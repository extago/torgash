# Demo data — catalogue and pictures (draft for approval, 2026-10-20)

What a fresh shop shows when "Demo data" is ticked at install (PLAN §55).
Real brands and models, our own words; every record the pack creates is
marked, so "Remove demo data" removes exactly it. Every demo product wears the
"Demo" label — and can be bought: the owner tries the cart and checkout on them.

Pictures go to `database/demo/images/<file>` — **JPG**, square, white or
light background, 1200–1600 px on the long side, quality ~85 (a product photo
comes out at 50–150 KB; the pack ships inside the distribution). Brand logos
are the one exception: PNG with transparency. A missing file is not an error:
the product shows the usual "no image" until it arrives.

## Everything around them

- **Characteristics** for the filter panel: brand, colour, built-in memory,
  RAM, screen size, processor, operating system, connectivity, battery,
  weight. Yes/no flags: 5G, NFC, MagSafe.
- **Brands** with logos (logo files: `brand-<slug>.png`, transparent).
- **Labels**: New, Hit, Sale (automatic), Demo.
- **Promotions**: "Deal of the day" with a countdown (one phone), "Smartphone
  week" (−10% on a category, a period), a coupon `DEMO10`.
- **Options and services**: laptop RAM/SSD upgrade, extended warranty 1/2
  years, setup at home for TV (by formula), gift wrap.
- **Reviews**: 2–4 per marked product, with criteria; text our own.
- **Blog**: 4 articles (how to choose headphones, phone comparison,
  setting up Wi-Fi at home, a gift guide), pictures `blog-<slug>.jpg`
  (landscape, 1600×900).
- **Pages**: About us, Delivery, Payment, Returns, Contacts, Privacy.
- **Home page**: main slider (3 banners, `banner-<n>.jpg`, 1920×800 or the
  template's size), the cards row (5–6 cards), category tiles, product tabs
  (Popular / New / On sale), latest articles.
- **Menus**: header strip (the categories), footer (pages).
- **Delivery, payment, warehouse**: one warehouse, pickup + courier,
  "cash on delivery" and "card online (test mode)".
